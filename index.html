About the foundation

SK Children Foundation provides free education to underprivileged children across 10+ Indian states. We believe every child deserves a place to learn, and we work to make that possible for families who could not otherwise afford it.

What we do

📚

Free Education

Access to schooling at no cost to the child's family.

Learning Support

Help that keeps children learning and growing with confidence.

🌱

Educational Opportunities

Openings that let children look ahead to a better future.

🤝

Community Support

Working alongside underserved communities to keep children in class.

Our mission

Education builds knowledge, confidence and skills that last a lifetime. Our mission is to put that within reach of children who are too often left out, in every state we work in.
